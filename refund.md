# Cancellation and Refund Policy

**Version:** 2026-10-05

## Cancelling
- You can cancel **at any time**, from your Whop account or through the "Manage subscription" link on the app's **Plan** screen.
- Cancellation takes effect at the **end of the current period**. You keep the Service until then, and nothing further is charged.
- **30 days** after the subscription ends, your cloud memory is permanently deleted. You can download or delete it in the app before then.

## Refunds

**Your first purchase: 14 days.** If this is your **first** Torvis subscription, you can ask for a refund within **14 days** of the first payment.
- The refund covers the **unused days of the period**. For example, if you ask on day 5 of a 30-day period, you get 25/30 of the price back and the subscription ends that day.
- This also covers the EU/UK right of withdrawal: by signing in you ask us to start the Service at once, and if you withdraw within 14 days you pay only for the days used.

**Later periods.** Renewed periods are not refunded. You can cancel at any time, effective at the end of the period.

**If the Service was unavailable through our fault** for a significant part of a period, we refund or extend the time you could not use.

**No refund** is given for accounts suspended or closed for breaking the Terms of Use.

**Your statutory rights are not affected.** This policy does not narrow any consumer rights you have by law.

## How to ask
Write to **[■ CONTACT EMAIL]** from the email you used to buy, with the last 4 characters of your licence key. We settle requests within **7 working days**. The refund goes back to your card through Whop; depending on your bank it can take a few days to arrive.
